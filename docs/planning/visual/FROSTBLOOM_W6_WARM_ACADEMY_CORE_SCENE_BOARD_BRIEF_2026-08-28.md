## Purpose

Create exactly one new planning board for the actual W6 learning-and-preservation experience. It must answer visually:

> A learner sees a local greenhouse problem, writes a spell, decides what to protect first, and understands the mixed result without being told there was a secret villain or a hidden correct answer.

The board is not a runtime asset, UI implementation, character approval, rights clearance, or Human/Device/Player Experience pass.

## Visual lock applied

- `GM-VISUAL-TONE-20260828-01` is mandatory: a warm, learner-centred academy practicum; curiosity, care, and growing competence come before mystery.
- Preserve Soft Storybook Cel 2D Hybrid, navy/aged-gold material, arched glasshouse, books, direct-written blue-to-violet magic, and warm lantern pools.
- Use a small non-identifiable young-adult learner viewpoint (hand, shoulder, or back) only. It must read as an early-twenties academy researcher through adult proportion and posture, not as a child or teen. No copied face, costume, mentor identity, hooded watcher, or character canon.
- The greenhouse stays breathable: clear blue night/dusk and warm local light, rather than a black void or a threatening purple fog.

## Five-panel composition

| Panel | Player question | Required visual | Must not imply |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 — observe | What needs care? | inviting academy glasshouse, frosted rare seedlings and a visibly strained glass/metal support; warm study corner and readable negative space | monster encounter, disaster city, hidden watcher |
| 02 — write | What can the learned glyph do? | close hand/book/direct luminous ink stroke; learner agency is clear | card/talisman collection metaphor |
| 03 — compose | What spell am I shaping? | directly written FIVE_POINT_STAR circuit on parchment/light, restrained and legible | old system UI, tokens inserted into slots, text labels |
| 04 — choose | What do I preserve first? | seedlings and greenhouse structure presented with equal size, value hierarchy, and light; no best-route marker | a hidden answer, combat target, hostile entity |
| 05 — reflect | What changed and what remains? | paired mixed consequences: recovering seedlings with a remaining structural crack, and stabilised structure with stressed seedlings; gentle Grimoire/record cue | score, victory screen, moral judgement, failure punishment |

Leave quiet lower/side reserves for future live UI without drawing buttons, copy, numbers, pseudo-text, or functional state.
